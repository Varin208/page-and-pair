Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Page & Pair is a student portfolio project that recommends books based on genre, trope, and mood preferences.

What we collect

If you create an account or sign in with Google, we store your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also store a unique identifier Google provides for your account. We store which books you've marked as "Already Read" or "Not Interested" so we can remember your preferences across visits.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data with third parties. We don't use your data for advertising. We don't access any Google data beyond your basic email and account identifier needed to sign you in.

Data storage

Your data is stored in a Supabase-hosted database used for this project. Passwords, when used, are stored as one-way hashes, never as plain text.
